From now on you can jot things down right here in Quick Notes. So what can you do with it?
Memory is not wisdom — but what wisdom is there without memory?
— Harper

Science says human memory has limits, yet life keeps handing us more to remember. That is the bad news. The good news: you no longer have to worry about it. Write a line in Quick Notes whenever it strikes you, add a calendar reminder, and nothing slips through. "Memory is not wisdom" — but using notes well to extend your memory is exactly where your wit shows. Reading takeaways, everyday moments, team plans: they all belong here. Star the notes that matter so they stand out the way this guide does. Finished notes can be shared to your favorite apps in one tap, or sent straight by email — quick and effortless. Quick Notes: for the small pieces of a life.

May this little tool make your days a bit lighter.
