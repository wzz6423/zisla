This adaptation retains the default quarantine-only processing, signature
and security-assessment checks, descriptor-based attribute removal, internal
symlink validation, before/after snapshots, and Launch Services opening.
It uses Zisla's existing subprocess runner and quick-action presentation.
Peck's UI, Go runtime, optional ad-hoc signing, and updater are not included.
